    How it works

    The same four steps, every working day, for every candidate.

    1. Your profile

      Send your resume, the roles you want, job types (W2, C2C, full-time) and where you live. We set up your account.

    2. Jobs matched

      New jobs arrive every hour and are scored against your resume. Jobs near you come first.

    3. We apply for you

      Your recruiter applies to the strongest matches and records each confirmation. Weak matches are skipped.

    4. Calls and next steps

      See every application and reply. Screens and interviews come with a prep sheet; replies get follow-ups.

    Questions

    Do you guarantee a job or interviews?

    No. Employers decide who they call, and anyone who guarantees that is not being straight with you. What we guarantee is the work: fresh matching jobs, careful applications, and a clear report of results.

    Who actually applies — me or you?

    Your Mahatrix recruiter applies on your behalf to jobs that match your profile, and records each confirmation. You can see every application in your account and tell us any company or role to avoid.

    Where do the jobs come from?

    From the employers' own career sites, at companies across the USA, plus vendor requirements sent to our recruiters. We check around the clock and add new jobs every hour.

    What about my work authorization?

    Tell us your status when you sign up. Jobs that say you can't apply — for example citizenship or a clearance required, or no visa sponsorship when you need it — are filtered out.

    Do you take a percentage of my salary when I get hired?

    No — never. There is no placement fee and no percentage of your pay, before or after you're hired. You only pay for your plan while we're working on your job search.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the plan — how many applications a day and how much resume and interview help you want. Message us and we'll share the details, or start with the 5-day free trial.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Plans are month to month. If you don't renew, work pauses at the end of the paid month and you can still sign in to see your past applications.

    What happens to my resume and details?

    They are used only to run your job search and are never sold. See our privacy policy, or email us to have your data deleted.

    Can my staffing firm use the platform with our own recruiters?

    Yes. Your team gets its own logins, and your candidates' data stays separate. Message us with your team size for a walkthrough and a quote.
