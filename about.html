About Mahatrix

Mahatrix Technologies is a U.S. staffing and job-search company. We combine software that finds and matches fresh jobs every hour with recruiters who know the IT market — so job seekers spend their time on the applications that can turn into calls.

Fresh over stale

Most screening calls come from applying in the first days a job is open. We find new jobs every hour and put them first.

Real experience, well told

Resumes are tailored from what a person has actually done — never invented employers, titles or dates.

Honest reporting

Every application and reply is in your account. We tell you what is working and what isn't.

What we promise

No one can honestly promise you a job. We promise the work: fresh jobs, careful applications, and an honest report of what happened.
  • No job or interview guarantees. Hiring decisions are the employer's.
  • Quality over volume. Weak matches are skipped unless there is a real reason to apply.
  • Your data is not for sale. We use it only to run your job search.
  • Nothing sent in your name without review. Follow-ups are checked by a person first.
