Questions

Do you guarantee a job or interviews?

No. Employers decide who they call, and anyone who guarantees that is not being straight with you. What we guarantee is the work: fresh matching jobs, careful applications, and a clear report of results.

Who applies — me or you?

On Launch you search and apply yourself on the company's own site, with tailored resumes ready in your account. On Autopilot, a dedicated Mahatrix recruiter applies for you every working day and records each application.

How does the free trial work?

You get 5 days free, with no card. Create your account with your email, confirm it, and you can apply to jobs from the search and tailor 2 resumes a day. Subscribe to Launch when you are ready.

How do I cancel?

Any time, online: open Billing on the jobs website or Manage billing in your account and cancel. There is no fee. You keep Launch until the end of the month you already paid for, and you are not charged again. See our refund and cancellation policy.
