Questions

Do you guarantee a job or interviews?

No. Employers decide who they call, and anyone who guarantees that is not being straight with you. What we guarantee is the work: fresh matching jobs, careful applications, and a clear report of results.

Who applies — me or you?

On Launch and Accelerate you apply yourself, with your matched jobs and tailored resumes ready in your account. On Autopilot, a dedicated Mahatrix recruiter applies for you every working day and records each application.

How does the free trial work?

You get 5 days of Launch, free, with no card. Create your account, upload your resume and your matched jobs appear right away. Choose a plan in your account when you are ready.

Why don't Accelerate and Autopilot show a price?

They are set up around your role, experience and goals. Send us a message or email and we reply with the details for you.
