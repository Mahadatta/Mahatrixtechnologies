Plan questions

What is the difference between Launch and Accelerate?

Both bring you full-time jobs matched to your resume, and you apply yourself. Accelerate gives you more fresh matches as they arrive, more tailored resumes each day and a skill-gap report. Message us and we will set it up for you.

What does Autopilot add?

A dedicated recruiter who applies for you, W2 and C2C contract jobs and vendor requirements as well as full-time jobs, unlimited tailored resumes, and interview preparation.

How does the free trial work?

You get 5 days of Launch, free, with no card. Create your account, upload your resume and your matched jobs appear right away. Choose a plan in your account when you are ready.

Why don't Accelerate and Autopilot show a price?

They are set up around your role, experience and goals. Send us a message or email and we reply with the details for you.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month. If you don't renew, the plan pauses at the end of the month and you can still sign in to see your past applications.

What happens to my resume and details?

They are used only to run your job search and are never sold. See our privacy policy, or email us to have your data deleted.
