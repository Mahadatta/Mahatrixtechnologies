    How it works

    The same four steps, every day, for every candidate.

    1. Your profile

      Send your resume, the roles you want, job types (W2, C2C, full-time) and where you live. We set up your login.

    2. Jobs found and matched

      New jobs from company career sites arrive every hour. Each one is scored against your resume, and jobs near you come first.

    3. We apply for you

      Your recruiter applies to the best matches and saves the confirmation email, number or page for each one. Low matches are skipped, not sprayed.

    4. Results and next steps

      You see every application and what happened. Replies get follow-ups, and screens and interviews come with a prep sheet.

    Questions

    Do you guarantee a job or interviews?

    No. We can't control who employers call, and anyone who guarantees that is not being straight with you. What we guarantee is the work: fresh matching jobs, careful applications, and a clear report of results.

    Who actually applies — me or you?

    Your Mahatrix recruiter applies on your behalf to jobs that match your profile, and logs each one. You can see every application in your login and tell us any company or role to avoid.

    Where do the jobs come from?

    From the career sites of 4,000+ U.S. companies — the employers' own job pages — plus vendor requirements sent to our recruiters. We check them around the clock and add new jobs every hour.

    What about my work authorization?

    Tell us your status when you sign up. Jobs that say you can't apply — for example, citizenship or a clearance required, or no visa sponsorship when you need it — are filtered out.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Plans are month to month. If you don't renew, work pauses at the end of the paid month and you can still sign in to see your past applications.

    What happens to my resume and details?

    They are used only to run your job search and are never sold. See our privacy policy for details, or email us to have your data deleted.

    Can my staffing firm use the portal with our own recruiters?

    Yes. Your team gets its own logins, and your candidates' data stays separate from ours. Message us with your team size for a demo and a quote.
