Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Cancel any time, online

You can cancel your Launch subscription whenever you like, with no fee: choose Billing on jobs.mahatrixtechnologies.com, or Manage billing in your account. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you already paid for — you keep your plan until then, and you are not charged again.

Free trial

The 5-day free trial needs no card and is never charged. If you don't subscribe, it simply ends.

Refunds

  • Monthly fees cover the month ahead, so we don't refund part of a month after you cancel.
  • We refund in full if you were charged by mistake (for example twice for the same month), or if a problem on our side stopped you from using the service for a large part of the month.
  • If you forgot to cancel and have not used the service since the renewal, email us within 7 days of the charge and we will refund that renewal.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within 5–10 business days.

Nothing in this policy limits any refund rights you have under the law where you live.

How to ask

Email info@mahatrixtechnologies.com from your account email with the date of the charge. We reply within 2 business days.
