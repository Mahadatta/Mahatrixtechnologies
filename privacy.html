Privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy explains what Mahatrix Technologies ("Mahatrix", "we") collects when you use mahatrixtechnologies.com, our job search website jobs.mahatrixtechnologies.com and the Mahatrix portal, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email and the role you are looking for, and a password or PIN. Passwords and PINs are stored only in a one-way scrambled form — nobody at Mahatrix can read them.
  • Sign-up and contact details you send us: phone, work authorization, years of experience, city and state, LinkedIn link and your message.
  • Your resume and job-search profile: resume text and file, target roles, job types and locations.
  • Job search activity: when you are signed in, the jobs you open to apply to, so your account can show your history. Searches by visitors who are not signed in are not linked to a person.
  • Application records: the jobs applied to, the confirmation of each application (for example a confirmation email or number), and outcomes such as screens, interviews or rejections.
  • Payment information: card payments are handled by Stripe, our payment provider. We receive your subscription status, payment dates and amounts, and the card brand and last four digits. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Usage and website data: sign-in times and basic activity, to keep accounts secure and the service working; and standard server logs kept by our hosting providers (such as IP address and browser type). We store only a one-way scrambled form of the IP address with messages and sign-ups, to stop spam.

Cookies and browser storage

We keep you signed in by saving a sign-in token in your browser's storage. We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers.

How we use it

  • To run your job search: show and match jobs, tailor your resume using only facts already in it, and — on Autopilot — apply on your behalf and report results to you.
  • To confirm your email, reply to your messages and run your account.
  • To bill your plan, send receipts and renewal notices, and handle cancellations.
  • To keep the service secure and improve it.

Who we share it with

We share your details with an employer or staffing vendor only when we apply or submit you for a job on your behalf; when you apply yourself, you send your details to the employer on their own site. We use service providers to run Mahatrix — cloud hosting (Google Cloud), email, and payment processing (Stripe) — who may process data only to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while you have an account and for a reasonable time after, unless you ask us to delete it. Tailored resumes are kept for 90 days. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Website messages and sign-ups that don't become accounts are deleted when no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or stop us applying for you, at any time by emailing info@mahatrixtechnologies.com. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights; we will honor them.

Security

Data is stored with a major cloud provider, every account needs a personal sign-in, and staff see only the candidates assigned to them. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password or PIN you don't use anywhere else.

Children

Mahatrix is for job seekers aged 18 and over.

Changes and contact

We will post changes to this page and update the date above. Questions: info@mahatrixtechnologies.com.
